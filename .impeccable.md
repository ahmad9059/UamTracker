## Design Context

### Users
UAM University students use the product to calculate GPA/CGPA, track semesters and courses, and understand academic progress. Admin users need fast oversight of student adoption, academic health, active sessions, and GPA data quality.

### Brand Personality
Clear, academic, and trustworthy. The interface should feel calm, organized, and confidence-building, with enough visual polish to make progress tracking feel encouraging rather than bureaucratic.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the existing product design system: Manrope headings, Source Sans 3 body text, a blue academic palette, soft aurora/glass surfaces, rounded cards, subtle shadows, and light/dark theme support. Avoid introducing a separate admin visual language.

### Design Principles
- Keep academic data legible before decorative.
- Use product vocabulary: students, semesters, courses, GPA, CGPA, credit hours, quality points.
- Surface risk and progress clearly without alarmist language.
- Reuse existing UI primitives, tokens, and layout patterns.
- Prefer safe, read-only administration unless destructive actions are explicitly approved.
